# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 6, 2026

**Roasting Plant** ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application, **Roasting Plant** (the "App").

---

### 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our App, we may collect and process the following types of information:

* **Information You Provide Directly:** Account details (e.g., name, email address, phone number), loyalty/rewards profile information, and customer support inquiries.
* **Order and Payment Details:** Information required to process mobile orders, coffee menu customizations, pickup selections, or transactions (payment card processing is handled securely by third-party processors).
* **Automatically Collected Data:** Technical data including your device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and app usage activity.
* **Location Data:** If enabled, precise or approximate location information to help you locate nearby Roasting Plant cafes and estimate order pickup times.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

* Fulfill and manage mobile coffee orders, pickup requests, and rewards/loyalty programs.
* Provide, maintain, and improve the features and performance of the App.
* Communicate order updates, customer service responses, and promotional offers (if opted-in).
* Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or fraudulent activity.

---

### 3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share necessary data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our App and delivering our services (e.g., payment gateways, cloud hosting, analytics tools, and mobile ordering integrations). These third parties are granted access to your data only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

---

### 4. Data Retention and Security

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. We maintain appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

---

### 5. Account and Data Deletion

In compliance with Apple's App Store Guidelines, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

To request account or data deletion:

* Use the in-app option: Navigate to **Account Settings > Delete Account**.
* Or contact us directly at **anwargelan707@icloud.com**.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt action to remove the information.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
